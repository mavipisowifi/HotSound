Settings

Applies whenever a sound is stopped: pressing a key again, or Stop all. 0 ms cuts it instantly, which can click on a loud sample.

On, a key toggles: press once to play, again to stop. Off, every press starts another copy, so rapid hits overlap (useful for drums).
